Calculation formulas

These formulas decide how many vaccine doses and how many Blue and Red Vitamin A capsules each team needs. Every team's Operational Plan uses them automatically. Only an administrator or the UCMO can change them.

Polio vaccine

Vaccine doses required = total children × this number (1.11 includes the usual wastage).

Red capsules

Capsules are worked out only for campaigns where Vitamin A capsules are marked Yes. A fraction is always rounded up. “Children 1–5 years” means household children aged 1 to 5 plus school and MMP children; “under 1 year” is the household under-1 figure of the plan.
