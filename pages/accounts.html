Login accounts — Area Incharges & team leaders

Each person signs in with their 13-digit CNIC as the username and the same CNIC as the first password. Passwords are not changed by users; only an administrator can reset them. Only an administrator or the UCMO of the union council can create, reset or disable these logins. Each team has one login only — its team leader (the leader set in Staff Management); other team members do not get logins. If a team's leader is changed, disable the old leader's login and create one for the new leader. A login needs a valid 13-digit CNIC in Staff Management.
